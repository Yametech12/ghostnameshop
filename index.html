Garment Care & Archive FAQ

What makes GHOSTNAME fabric distinct?

Our hoodies and boxy tees are milled from 500 GSM loopback French terry and 320 GSM combed jersey cotton in Wakayama, Japan, using vintage sinker-wheel machines that preserve natural yarn elasticity.

How do the garments fit?

Pieces feature a wide, dropped-shoulder boxy drape with a slightly cropped hemline. We recommend selecting your true size for the intended oversized architectural fit, or sizing down one for a closer profile.

Are sold-out archive pieces ever restocked?

Never. Each collection is produced in a single run of 500 pieces per style. Once an item enters "SOLD OUT" status in the Archive, the pattern is permanently retired to ensure collector scarcity.

Recommended Washing & Longevity

Turn garment inside out. Machine wash on delicate cold cycle (max 30°C / 85°F). Flat air dry in shaded breeze. Never tumble dry or bleach. Iron low on reverse.
