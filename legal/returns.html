Returns & Refunds

14-Day Vault Standard

Window

Every release is limited to 500 numbered units, so we hold a strict 14-day return window from the delivery date. Items after that window may be offered vault credit at our discretion.

Eligibility

  • Unworn and unwashed, with original tags attached
  • Tamper-evident woven security loop intact
  • Original dust bags and hangtags included

Process

Email concierge@ghostnameworldwide.com with your order number. A prepaid DHL return airway bill is generated immediately — repack, attach, drop off. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 48 hours of inspection, in the currency you were charged.

Exchanges

If sizing needs adjusting and inventory allows, exchanges are prioritized before refunds. Archive pieces marked SOLD OUT cannot be restocked or exchanged once retired.

Damaged or incorrect item? Send a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we make it right — return label on us, replacement dispatched same week.
