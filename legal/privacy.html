Privacy Policy

Anonymous by design

The Short Version

Your browsing trail, wishlist and cart live in your browser's local storage — on your device, under your control. We run no advertising pixels, no cross-site trackers, no data-broker integrations. Analytics stay switched off until you explicitly accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your mind anytime via Cookie Preferences in the footer.

What We Store

  • On your device (essential): cart contents, wishlist, currency preference, recently viewed items — via browser local storage.
  • At our payment processor (Snipcart): only what a receipt requires — ordered items, shipping address, contact email. Card details are entered on Snipcart's PCI-DSS compliant checkout and never touch our servers.
  • In our newsletter system: your email address, solely to send drop alerts. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Optional analytics (only with consent): aggregated, cookieless page-view counts via Plausible — no personal profiles, no cross-site tracking.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar regulations you may request access, correction, export or deletion of your data at any time by writing to privacy@ghostnameworldwide.com. Clearing your browser storage wipes everything stored on your device instantly.

Third Parties

We share data only with the processors required to run the store: our payment processor (order fulfilment) and our email platform (if you subscribe). We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information — ever.

Faceless means faceless. We can't sell what we never collect.
