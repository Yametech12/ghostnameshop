Shipping Policy

Effective FW26 · Worldwide

Dispatch

All orders are packed in insured, tamper-evident matte black boxes and dispatched from our Tokyo distribution atelier within 2 business days of payment confirmation. Tracking is emailed the moment your parcel leaves the vault.

Transit Estimates

  • Japan & East Asia — 1–2 business days (Yamato Transport)
  • North America — 2–3 business days (DHL Express Priority)
  • Europe & United Kingdom — 3–4 business days (FedEx International)
  • Rest of world — 3–5 business days (DHL Global Express)

Rates

Orders above $150 USD ship free worldwide. Below that, a flat rate is calculated at checkout based on destination. Charges appear in the currency selected in the storefront before checkout.

Duties & Tariffs

We ship DDP (Delivery Duty Paid): international customs duties and clearance fees are pre-calculated and covered by GHOSTNAME. You will never receive a surprise invoice at your door.

Questions? Contact concierge@ghostnameworldwide.com — the atelier answers within one business day.
