Shipping Policy

Effective FW26 · Worldwide

Made to Order

Every GHOSTNAME piece is produced on demand: printing, stitching, and packing begin the moment your order is confirmed. Production takes 2–5 business days before shipping begins.

Production & Transit

  • Production, 2–5 business days, every item is made on demand
  • Transit, tracked worldwide shipping after production; timing varies by destination
  • Tracking, the carrier tracking link becomes available once your order has shipped

Rates

Orders above $150 USD ship free worldwide. Below that, a flat $12 USD rate applies at checkout. All charges are made in USD.

Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to import duties and local taxes, set by your customs authority. These charges are not included in your order total and are the recipient's responsibility.
